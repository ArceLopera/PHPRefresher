# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

PHPRefresher is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We are dedicated to creating an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued, respected, and empowered to contribute.

---

## Our Values

### 🤝 Respect
We treat each other with kindness, dignity, and respect. We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds.

### 🌍 Inclusion
We welcome people of all backgrounds, experiences, and identities. Everyone belongs here.

### 📚 Collaboration
We work together to build something greater than any individual could alone.

### 💡 Learning
We embrace mistakes as learning opportunities and support each other's growth.

### 🎯 Excellence
We strive for high quality in our work and our interactions.

---

## Expected Behavior

All members of the PHPRefresher community are expected to:

### ✅ Do This

- **Be Welcoming**: Greet new contributors warmly and help them get started
- **Be Respectful**: Listen to others' viewpoints with an open mind
- **Be Constructive**: Provide helpful feedback that builds people up
- **Be Inclusive**: Use inclusive language and avoid assumptions
- **Be Patient**: Remember that everyone learns at their own pace
- **Be Professional**: Maintain high standards in your interactions
- **Give Credit**: Acknowledge others' contributions and ideas
- **Ask Questions**: When you don't understand something, ask politely

### Examples of Good Behavior

> "I like your approach! Have you considered...?"
> "Great contribution! I learned something new from this."
> "I'm new here. Could someone help me understand...?"
> "I disagree with your approach, but I appreciate you sharing your perspective."

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

### ❌ Don't Do This

- **Harassment**: Unwelcome or hostile behavior directed at any person
- **Discrimination**: Treating people unfairly based on identity characteristics
- **Trolling**: Intentionally disrupting conversations or being provocative
- **Attacks**: Personal or ad hominem attacks on individuals
- **Exclusion**: Deliberately excluding people or groups
- **Intimidation**: Threatening behavior or language
- **Abuse**: Any form of verbal, physical, or sexual abuse
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Spam**: Repeated unsolicited messages or content

### Examples of Bad Behavior

> "Your code is garbage. You must be incompetent."
> "People like you don't belong in tech."
> "I'm going to find out where you live."
> "Your contribution is worthless. Don't bother."

## Conflict Resolution

### If You Experience Unacceptable Behavior

1. **Document It**: Note what happened, when, and who was involved
2. **Report It**: Contact project maintainers privately
3. **Be Specific**: Share details to help us understand the situation

**How to Report:**
- Open a private issue on GitHub
- Email project maintainers
- Contact through other appropriate channels

### Investigation Process

When we receive a report:

1. **Acknowledge**: We confirm receipt of your report
2. **Investigate**: We gather information from all involved parties
3. **Determine**: We assess whether the Code of Conduct was violated
4. **Respond**: We take appropriate action

### Potential Outcomes

Depending on the severity and nature of the violation:

- **Conversation**: A private discussion with the involved person(s)
- **Warning**: A clear statement that behavior is unacceptable
- **Temporary Ban**: Removal from community for a defined period
- **Permanent Ban**: Removal from community permanently

---

## For Maintainers

### Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- ✅ Enforcing this Code of Conduct consistently
- ✅ Investigating reports fairly and thoroughly
- ✅ Protecting reporter confidentiality
- ✅ Responding promptly to concerns
- ✅ Setting a positive example
- ✅ Being accessible and approachable

### Actions We Will Take

When unacceptable behavior is identified:

1. **Address the behavior**: Directly but respectfully
2. **Document the incident**: For record-keeping
3. **Follow up**: Ensure behavior change
4. **Escalate if needed**: Involve additional maintainers for serious cases

## Building a Better Community

### How You Can Help

Even if you're not a maintainer, you can support our community:

- **Welcome** new members and help them get started
- **Correct gently** when you see missteps
- **Celebrate** contributions and wins
- **Stand up** respectfully against unacceptable behavior
- **Lead by example** in your own interactions
- **Give feedback** on how we can improve

### Creating an Inclusive Environment

To help everyone feel welcome:

- Use inclusive language (avoid gendered pronouns when not needed)
- Respect different perspectives and working styles
- Be patient with different skill levels
- Acknowledge that people have different backgrounds
- Assume good intent, but address impact
- Create space for quiet voices to be heard
